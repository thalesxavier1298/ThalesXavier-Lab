✦Super Mushroom

Hit a ? box while Small to release a green mushroom and grow. Special power → Super → Small → retry when hit.

☁Cloud flower

Create up to three temporary cloud platforms. Only another Cloud flower refills your three charges. Entering a new stage also restores three charges. Cloud trials require aerial platforms. At a trial, C or Retry cloud trial resets its supplies without losing a life.

✹Fire flower

Launch bouncing embers to defeat enemies and damage bosses.

❄Ice flower

Freeze rivals for six seconds. E/J picks up or drops a nearby ice block; X/K throws it: ice slides until it hits a wall, lava or a resistant target, or falls out of the stage. It can defeat several ordinary rivals. E/J drops it without launching. Carrying and sliding pause thawing. Stand on frozen rivals safely; hold Down and press X/K to break nearby ice. Fixed plants cannot be carried. The Sentinel resists ice.

⚒Hammer flower

A hammer-shaped blossom. Throw arcing hammers over obstacles and into enemies. Fire and Hammer hurt the Sentinel; Ice does not.

✣Helicopter mushroom

Boost upward with Power; hold Jump to slow your fall.

5 LIVES · A GENEROUS TIMER

Falling, taking damage while Small, or running out of time costs one life. Retry at your checkpoint with a fresh timer. At Game Over, return to the map with five lives. Every 50 coins earns an extra life; coins carry between stages. One green mushroom is hidden in each stage. Pause to quit a stage; re-entering starts from the beginning. Cleared stages remain saved.

New rivals: stomp shellbacks to stop them. E/J picks up or drops a stationary shell; X/K throws it. You can also touch a stationary shell to kick it. Moving shells can hurt Jarvis; stomp again to stop them. Winged rivals patrol overhead, while ember buds warn before firing. Ember scouts fire after a warning; iron throwers hurl hammers; leapers jump and shoot. Carnivorous buds open and close: wait or jump over.
