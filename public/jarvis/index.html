5 LIVES · A GENEROUS TIMER

Falling, taking damage while Small, or running out of time costs one life. Retry at your checkpoint with a fresh timer. At Game Over, return to the map with five lives. Every 50 coins earns an extra life; coins carry between stages. One green mushroom is hidden in each stage. Pause to quit a stage; re-entering starts from the beginning. Cleared stages remain saved.

New rivals: ember scouts fire after a warning; iron throwers hurl hammers; leapers jump and shoot. Carnivorous buds open and close: wait or jump over. Freeze a rival to shatter it; armored rivals take two ordinary hits. The Storm King jumps between arena platforms: watch the landing marker, then counterattack.
